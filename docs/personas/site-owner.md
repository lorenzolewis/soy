## Who they are
Lorenzo is a software engineer with an international life and a long trail of jobs, open-source work and projects *(Confirmed)*. He reaches for the site when he needs to recall or point to something he has done *(Confirmed)*.

## Goals
- Have a single place to refer to a list of what he has done in his life *(Confirmed)*
- Showcase his technical skills, work history, projects and the places he has lived *(Confirmed)*

## Pain points
- His history is spread across a CV, repos and memory, so there is no one complete list *(Assumed)*

## What this means for the product
- Everything on the site is public. It is an "About me", not a journal: no private or hidden entries *(Confirmed)*.
- The record covers work, projects, skills and places lived. Travel is out of scope *(Confirmed)*.
- Favour completeness over curation: the full record should exist somewhere on the site, even if the front page highlights only the best of it.
- Keep content in structured data (one entry per job, project, place), so adding a new item is a quick edit, not a page redesign.
- Every fact must be true: never invent dates, figures or details. If something is missing, ask Lorenzo rather than fill it in *(Confirmed)*.
- Don't frame Lorenzo around one specialism (data, automation, a particular stack). Keep the positioning broad until he says otherwise *(Confirmed)*.
