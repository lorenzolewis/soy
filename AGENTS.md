# AGENTS.md

## About the site

- Fully public: an "About me" page.
- Covers work, projects, skills and places Lorenzo has lived.
- Technical detail supports the story of who Lorenzo is and what he has done.
- Keep Lorenzo's positioning broad until he narrows it.
- Visually striking, with the design serving the content.
- Every fact must be true and sourced from Lorenzo; ask him when something is missing.

## User personas

The people this site is for are described in `docs/personas/`:

- [Site owner](docs/personas/site-owner.md): Lorenzo himself, using the site as a single place listing everything he has done in his life.
- [Professional visitor](docs/personas/professional-visitor.md): a technical person interested in knowing more about Lorenzo, and a potential professional connection.

Before designing a feature, writing user-facing text, or asking the user about functionality, read the relevant persona. Pitch complexity and wording at that person, and frame questions in terms of their goals ("would an engineer browsing this want to get in touch?"). Base decisions on lines marked *(Confirmed)*. Lines marked *(Assumed)* are unverified guesses; ask the user before letting one decide a design choice.
